# Capability Maturity and Firm Performance Across Manufacturing and IT Industries

This repository contains supporting materials and analysis for the paper *“An Empirical Examination of the Relationship Between Capability Maturity and Firm Performance Across Manufacturing and IT Industries”* published in *Management and Production Engineering Review*.

## Paper

- **Title:** An Empirical Examination of the Relationship Between Capability Maturity and Firm Performance Across Manufacturing and IT Industries  
- **Authors:** L. Hayes, J. Lu, & D. Rezania  
- **Journal:** *Management and Production Engineering Review*, Vol. 13 (2022)  
- **DOI:** [https://doi.org/10.24425/mper.2022.142055](https://doi.org/10.24425/mper.2022.142055)  
- **Ranking:** Unranked

## In plain language

This study explores whether firms with more mature management capabilities—such as standardized processes, performance tracking, and continuous improvement—achieve stronger performance outcomes.  
Drawing on survey and performance data from both manufacturing and IT firms, we find that higher capability maturity correlates with better efficiency and profitability.  
However, the strength of this relationship differs across industries, suggesting that capability maturity contributes to performance in context-dependent ways.
